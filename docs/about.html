About Gadget Gift Guide

Who we are, how we choose what goes in our gift guides, and how the site makes money.

Updated October 17, 2026

How we pick

Gadget Gift Guide publishes practical gift guides for tech and everyday gadgets. Each guide starts from a real buying situation - a budget, a person or a hobby - and lists products that solve a specific everyday problem for that situation.

  • We favor widely available products and well-established product categories over novelty items.
  • Every pick comes with a short, plain-English explanation of why it made the list and, where it matters, a compatibility note (for example, whether a gadget needs an iPhone).
  • We haven't hands-on tested every product we list. Rather than inventing scores, we describe what a product does and who it suits.
  • We don't publish star ratings, review counts, testimonials or prices, because those change constantly and we can't verify them. Check the current details on the retailer's page before buying.
  • We update guides when products change or better options become widely available. Each guide shows the date it was last updated.

Affiliate disclosure

Gadget Gift Guide is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

That means if you click a link on this site and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Links to Amazon are marked as sponsored links. Commissions help keep the site running; they never determine which products we include, and no brand pays to be listed.

Privacy

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites. When you click through to Amazon, Amazon's own privacy policy applies on their site.
